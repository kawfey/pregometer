Pregometer

Compare the test line on home pregnancy tests across several days. Add your photos and get a score for each test, matched side-by-side crops, and a report you can save.

Your photos stay on this device. The analysis runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Photograph from straight above, in focus, in even light without glare.
  • Fill a good part of the frame with the test. Tests far in the background are ignored.
  • Use the original photo where you can. Screenshots and copies sent through chat apps lose the date and some detail.
  • Read tests within the time window in their instructions. A line that appears after a test has dried is not a result.

2. Check the dates

Rows in the report are ordered by these. Dates come from the photo where it has one; fill in any that are missing.

    Not a medical device. It measures how dark a line looks in a photo, which is not the same as a hormone level. See the limits listed in the report.

    Runs on Pyodide, NumPy, OpenCV, Pillow and libheif, all served from this site. No analytics, no cookies.
